## 2. Strategic Framing — Why Land Now

1. **Longest lead time, hardest to parallelize later.** Title, zoning, environmental, and water-licensing timelines in Ghana commonly run 6–18 months and gate everything else. Starting now buys that runway for free.
2. **Option value, not commitment.** A shortlist + term-sheet *options* cost only diligence effort (and <$1K for surveys/agent fees if we later approve them). We can walk away at any point before signing.
3. **Protects the funding narrative.** Investors/grants asked "have you secured a site?" want "shortlisted + due-diligence in flight," not "site TBD." It de-risks the $1.0–$1.46M Wave-1 ask (CEO-signed, 2026-09-10).
4. **Feeds two other tracks simultaneously** — the buildout brief needs real site parameters (slope, water source, solar irradiance, access road) to be credible; funding decks need a concrete story.

**What we explicitly will NOT do yet:** no purchase, no deposits, no construction contracts, no money above $1,000. All gated on CEO sign-off.

## 3. Site Selection Scoring Rubric

Score each candidate site 0–5 per criterion. **Weighted total /100.** Sites must pass every **hard gate** first (binary yes/no); only then rank by weighted score.

### Hard gates (all must be YES to be scored)
| # | Gate | Why |
|---|------|-----|
| H1 | ≥ **28 hectares** achievable (or 28 + expansion headroom) | 28 units + labs + studio + clinic + market + farm |
| H2 | **Water source on/near site**: existing stream/waterfall or a viable borehole with confirmed yield | 100% water self-sufficiency |
| H3 | **No existing third-party encumbrance** that cannot be cleared (unresolved boundary dispute, active litigation) | title risk |
| H4 | **Legal pathway to registered title** exists (see §4) | without this there is no investable asset |
| H5 | **Access road to a main road** feasible (or fundable) | logistics, Sprynter bus access, residents' connectivity |

### Weighted scoring rubric
| Weight | Criterion | 5 (ideal) signals |
|--------|-----------|-------------------|
| 15 | **Water self-sufficiency** | perennial stream/waterfall on site, spring, + spare borehole yield; treatment distance short |
| 15 | **Solar potential & shading** | open southern-ish aspect, minimal tall-tree canopy over array zone, irradiance data, no major ridge shading |
| 12 | **Access & connectivity** | paved/graded road to a town within ~30–45 min; reliable electricity grid as backup; mobile signal / broadband option |
| 12 | **Land cost & title clarity** | clear freehold/registered path, low per-acre cost, no stool/family complications, no litigation |
| 10 | **Terrain & build cost** | gentle slope for 28 units + flat pads for labs/solar; low rock/landslide risk; drainage good |
| 8 | **Eco / climate fit** | microclimate near waterfalls (stated vision), soil for on-farm food, low flood/erosion |
| 8 | **Community & security** | amicable neighbors/chiefs, low crime, local labor availability, water-sharing goodwill |
| 7 | **Proximity to Pricewards & Sprynter hubs** | within reach of production partners + first Sprynter city for the closed loop |
| 3 | **Expansion optionality** | room to grow units beyond 28, adjacent parcels, zoning flexibility |

**Decision rule:** pick the highest weighted score that passes all hard gates. If two are within 5 points, prefer the one with **cleaner title** (title risk is the least reversible).

**Output the CEO gets:** a ranked shortlist table (3–5 sites) with hard-gate pass/fail, weighted score, and a one-line "why it wins / why it loses."

## 4. Ghana Land Due-Diligence Checklist (verify with local counsel — I am not a Ghanaian lawyer)

> These are the *questions and documents to collect*, not legal advice. Flag any "cannot confirm" for retention of a Ghana land/property attorney before signing anything.

**Tenure type & ownership**
- [ ] Is the land **state land**, **stool land**, **family (clan) land**, or **company/private** land? (~Majority of Ghana land is customary — stool or family.)
- [ ] If **stool/family land**: consent of the stool/skirmish head + the relevant family heads, documented; verify the signers are the lawful title holders (letters of authority, family register).
- [ ] If **state land**: disposition only via the **President/Lands Commission** — confirm route and any national-land premium.
- [ ] Full chain of ownership to present owner; any gifts, inheritances, or pending claims.

**Title & registration**
- [ ] **Land Title Certificate** / Indenture registered — obtain certified copies.
- [ ] Search at the **Lands Commission** registry for the plot: registered owner, encumbrances, mortgages, restrictions, caveats, litigation.
- [ ] **Deed of Conveyance / Indenture** + Stamp Duty paid (Lands Commission + GRA).
- [ ] Survey: **Survey Plan** signed by a licensed surveyor, registered; confirm 28-hectare boundary pegs and area match the title exactly.
- [ ] **Land Act 2020 (Act 1036)** compliance — registration, transfer, and any consent requirements.

**Planning, environment, water (sustainability-critical)**
- [ ] **Building permit** pathway via the **District/Municipal Assembly** — confirm the assembly allows the intended use (residential + industrial/lab + agribusiness + clinic).
- [ ] Zoning / **Land Use Plan** for the plot; confirm no protected-area / riparian-buffer conflict (waterfalls + streams often have riparian setback rules).
- [ ] **EPA Ghana** — Environmental Impact Assessment (EIA) / registration requirement at scale; forestry clearance if the site has trees (Ghana Forestry Commission).
- [ ] Water / borehole license — Ghana Water Authority / DWA abstraction permit; confirm a yield test is possible and any community water-sharing agreement.
- [ ] Any **EPA waste** handling plan for the lab/production (sewing, bus workshop) — must be part of the 100% sustainability brief.

**Commercial**
- [ ] Per-acre or per-plot price; **option/term-sheet** (not purchase) — with a long enough option period to complete diligence + funding.
- [ ] Any **development rights transfer** cost, stool development levy, or community development contribution (common and material — budget for it).

**Deal-structures to run before committing**
- [ ] Option-to-purchase with **diligence contingency** + **title-certainty condition precedent** (walk away if the title search fails).
- [ ] Escrow of any deposit until title search + survey + EIA clear.
- [ ] A **title-insurance / legal-opinion** deliverable from local counsel before money moves.

## 5. Phase 3 Buildout Outline (M18–36, sustainability-gated)

Sizing from the business plan. Each phase has a **sustainability gate** that must pass before the next begins (100% solar + water self-sufficiency is non-negotiable per value #2).

| Phase | Window (M) | Scope | Plan budget | Sustainability gate |
|-------|-----------|-------|------------|---------------------|
| P0 — Site & permits | 18–20 | Land option + full §4 diligence; site plan; EIA; water/solar survey | $50–100K | Title clean + water source confirmed |
| P1 — Sustain core | 20–24 | Borehole/treatment water system; **solar plant sized for full 28-unit + lab + AI load** (not phased); access road | $100K | Solar plant capacity ≥ peak site load + buffer; water treatment to potable |
| P2 — Housing | 20–30 | 28 modular housing units (expandable) | $250–350K | Units run on on-site solar/water; construction waste plan |
| P3 — Production labs | 24–30 | Sewing (Pricewards), workshop (Sprynter), 3D-print, AI server room | $80–110K | AI server room cooling within solar budget; e-waste plan |
| P4 — Creative studios | 26–32 | Music, film, conference rooms | $70K | Powered from on-site grid |
| P5 — Public | 28–34 | Market, clinic, transport hub (Sprynter) | $50K | Clinic water/waste; market waste-to-value |
| P6 — AI infra | 24–28 | AI server room (Ollama/vLLM) for all 3 platforms | $30K | 24/7 load on solar; generator = backup only, not base |

**Key sustainability design posture (bakes value #2 into the brief):**
- Size **solar for peak load in P1**, before housing exists — otherwise retrofitting power across 28 units is far costlier.
- Water: **dual-source** (surface stream/waterfall + borehole) with on-site treatment → resiliency for the 100% target.
- All three revenue-supporting labs (sewing / workshop / studio) colocate so one utility spine (power, water, waste) serves them → cheaper, lower footprint.

## 6. Decision Packet (for CEO sign-off)

**Why it matters:** Land is the longest-lead, hardest-to-reverse input to the entire $1.0–$1.46M Wave-1, break-even line. Starting diligence now costs little and protects the funding narrative for Village, Sprynter, and the Phase-1 raise.

**Options:**
- **A) Site selection + due-diligence now (recommended).** Cost: diligence effort + <$1K for agent/survey if approved. Risk: low. Reversible until signing. Protects timeline.
- **B) Full buildout brief now (without a site).** Costs attention, but a brief with no real site data (slope, water, irradiance) is weaker and risks rework.
- **C) Funding/grant pipeline first.** Valuable, but a funding deck is more credible *with* a shortlisted site; sequencing land first strengthens it.

**Recommendation (ship-and-iterate):** Do **A now**, run **D in parallel** (it's cheap and feeds investor conversations), and stand up **B/C as soon as a top-ranked candidate clears its hard gates** — because a real site makes both a credible brief and a stronger ask.
