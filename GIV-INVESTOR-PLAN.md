## THE PROBLEM

**Three problems. One company.**

**Problem 1: Custom clothing is broken.** A customer in Accra wants a custom-fit outfit. She must find a designer, visit a tailor, get measured, negotiate fabric, wait weeks — and the fit is often still wrong. No single platform connects designers, fabric suppliers, production, and delivery. She pays in cash, has no order tracking, and no quality guarantee.

**Problem 2: Public transport in Ghana is unreliable and unsafe.** The trotro system moves millions daily, but passengers have no guaranteed seats, unpredictable schedules, no digital payments, no real-time tracking. Women face safety concerns. Young people who want to own a transport business can't afford a vehicle, can't fill seats reliably, and can't maintain their vehicles.

**Problem 3: Makers and creators have nowhere to build.** Ghana's growing tech and creative community lacks a place to live, work, manufacture, prototype, and create in one self-sustaining location. Makers split their time and money across separate apartments, workshops, and studios.

## THE SOLUTION

### Project 1: Pricewards (launching first)

A custom clothing infrastructure platform connecting customers, designers, production partners, fabric suppliers, and logistics — in one app.

**How it works:** Customer inputs measurements → browses designer catalog → selects a design → a human designer customizes it to the customer's measurements → customer pays Pricewards → order goes to a production partner → production partner makes it → logistics partner delivers → Pricewards pays each partner their share and retains 15–20%.

**What makes it different:**
- Full lifecycle — measurement to delivery, not just a marketplace
- Serves everyday customers and fashion brands using it as a backend
- Production anywhere in the world via partner network
- Designer Studio (vector pattern tool, in final build) turns designers into active creators on-platform
- Digital first, physical measurement shops later

### Project 2: Sprynter (Phase 2)

Mobile-first SaaS for scheduled mini-bus transport: guaranteed seating, station-based booking, live GPS tracking, digital payments, and LUXURY on-demand rides (Uber-style, 1.8x fare tier).

**How it works:** Operator buys a Sprynter-branded bus (full payment or installment) → Sprynter installs the operator app → passengers book a guaranteed seat and pay digitally → bus runs scheduled routes with real-time tracking → free for operators Year 1 → 25% commission per ride from Year 2 → servicing included in installment → remote lock if installments stop.

**Fleet tiers:**

| Tier | Vehicle | Multiplier | 10 km fare example |
|------|---------|-----------|--------------------|
| STANDARD | Toyota Voxy / Noah Hybrid | 1.0x | GHS 70 |
| COMFORT | GAC M6 8-Seat | 1.15x | GHS 80.50 |
| LUXURY | Toyota Alphard Hybrid | 1.8x | GHS 126 |

**What makes it different:**
- Vehicle ownership model — operators own a business, not a shift
- Servicing included — recurring relationship, fleet quality, parts access
- Guaranteed seating + digital payments + live GPS
- Three-tier pricing (Standard / Comfort / Luxury) lets riders choose their price class
- Remote lock protects the asset if installments fail

8. **The founder is the right person** — builds the software, maintains the buses, sells the vision, recruits the partners, and does the physical work. A rare combination, verified by shipping.
