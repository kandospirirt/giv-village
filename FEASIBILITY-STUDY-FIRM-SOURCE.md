## 0. How the regulatory frame works in Ghana (context, why it matters)

- **Engineering Council of Ghana** (est. Engineering Council Act 2011, **Act 819**; Regs **L.I. 2410**) is the body that licenses **individual engineers** AND **engineering firms**. Its own FAQ confirms *"engineering firms need to be registered"* and Act 819 §33 restricts firm operation to one with a registered engineer. It is a **separate** body from the professional **PECG/GhIE** (Ghana Institution of Engineering); the "PECG / PEGC" reference commonly used maps to the **Engineering Council** register for firm + individual licensing. Verify any candidate on the Engineering Council firm portal (engcouncil.org) or via info@engcouncil.gov.gh / +233 30 397 5228. *(Source: engcouncil.gov.gh, retrieved 2026-09-11.)*
- **Energy Commission of Ghana** (Act 541; Renewable Energy Act 2011, **Act 832**) licenses renewable-energy **service providers** — this is where the **solar EPC/installation** licensing lives, and the register is **public and searchable** (license numbers visible). For the 120–200 kW study the key credential is a **Wholesale/Generation or Installation & Maintenance license** (for 2 MW+ generation, a project-specific wholesale supply licence; installation/maintenance for smaller C&I). *SunPower's EC/IM license below is verifiable publicly.* *(Source: energycom.gov.gh licensing registers, retrieved 2026-09-11.)*
- **Water Resources Commission (WRC)**: drilling requires a WRC permit and a **driller registered with the WRC**. For a **feasibility** (not a build), the firm's hydrogeological survey + **pump/yield test** is what de-risks the borehole; a WRC-registered driller is engaged only if the study moves into a test drill. *(Source: Ownkey Ghana borehole guide, 2026-09-11; CWSA hydrogeology staff profile, 2026-09-11.)*

**Why this matters for the CEO:** you can (and should) **independently verify** each candidate's firm license on the Engineering Council portal and any solar firm on the Energy Commission register **before** you send money — both registers are public. That is the cheapest, highest-leverage de-risking step available and it costs nothing.

## 3. Overlap vs. L1 (land diligence line) — no double-billing, here's why

Per **D2-FIRST-WAVE-PLAN.md §2 L1** ("Land option + diligence + permits," $50K–$100K), L1 **already bundles "water + solar feasibility studies"** — but L1's are **site-specific, post-land-option** (run on the one specific plot in Eastern or Volta **after** the land option is won).

**This study is different and deliberately scoped to avoid double-billing:**
- **Different purpose:** this is a **pre-site-selection comparative** (which region — Eastern *vs* Volta — and is a 28 ha eco-village even plausible there?). L1's is **plot-specific detail** on the landed site.
- **Different depth:** this is **regional screening** (yield test at candidate locations, geotech zones, regional solar resource + distribution assumptions). L1's is **site-specific engineering** (exact borehole, exact foundation, exact PV array layout) that feeds G1/G2.
- **How to keep them separate in the budget:**
  1. **This study = "Regional Feasibility Screen" (new line, ~$8K–$15K).** Deliverable: go/no-go on region + indicative yields/geotech/solar → feeds the **land selection decision**.
  2. **L1's studies (inside $50K–$100K) = "Site-Specific Feasibility" (the won plot).** Deliverable: the G1 (solar ≥115% of peak) + G2 (water treatment at peak day + 2× storage) **engineering memos** the sustainability gates require.
  3. **Do NOT pay for the same borehole twice.** If this study runs a **pump/yield test** at a candidate plot, its data should **roll into L1** on the won plot — so the L1 water line should be scoped as *"site-specific design & confirmation (yield test data already available from the regional screen)"* to avoid re-paying for an identical test. **This is the one place a double-billing would actually happen, and it is avoidable by carrying the yield-test dataset forward.**

**Bottom line:** no true overlap in *purpose*; one **data-reuse** guardrail (carry the yield-test dataset into L1 so you don't re-drill/re-test the identical borehole). If the CEO confirms **single-region** scope, this risk nearly vanishes.
