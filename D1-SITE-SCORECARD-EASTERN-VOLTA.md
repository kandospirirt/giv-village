## 1. Scope — what "eastern + volta" means for scoring

Both regions sit within the same transport corridor (Accra — Tema — Eastern — Volta), but the two present very different site economics for a 28-acre, 100%-solar/water eco-village:

| Factor                        | eastern Region                                  | volta Region                                    |
|-------------------------------|-------------------------------------------------|-------------------------------------------------|
| Distance to Accra/Tema (lab, market, logistics hub)         | ~50–90 km (close) — better for Pricewards production loop  | ~100–150 km (farther) — better for quiet, lower-cost land |
| Known geography fits "mountains + waterfalls" (verified)     | Aburi / Akwapim hills (Eastern — Wikipedia-verified)      | Kpando + Aflao area (Volta — Wikipedia-verified); many mountain streams |
| Land cost tendency            | Higher (proximity to Accra)                      | Lower                                            |
| Power interconnection (grid)  | ECG distribution already dense                   | ECG distribution thinner in rural areas          |
| Mobile/broadband (for AI server room uplink) | Stronger                                          | Variable — must verify at specific site            |
| Road quality to a main axis (N1 Accra–Kumasi)                  | Generally better                                | Generally worse (pave/unpave mix)                  |

**The core trade-off to score:** eastern = closer to production/market at higher land cost, volta = cheaper and more remote at the cost of logistics. The rubric weights access/logistics at 12 pts and land cost/title clarity at 12 pts, so this is exactly where the score will separate.

## 3. Scoring rubric (repeated here for the scorecard table)

**Hard gates** (all must be YES to score):
- H1: ≥28 ha
- H2: Water source on/near site (stream/waterfall or borehole with confirmed yield)
- H3: No unresolved third-party encumbrance
- H4: Clear legal path to registered title
- H5: Access road feasible

**Weighted criteria** (0–5 each, total possible = 100):
15 water + 12 solar/shading + 10 access/connectivity + 12 land cost/title + 10 terrain/build cost + 8 eco/climate + 8 community/security + 7 proximity to Pricewards/Sprynet hubs + 3 expansion
