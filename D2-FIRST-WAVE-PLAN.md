## 1. Wave logic

**Wave 1 (M18–M30) — the right-sized ask here: ~$750K–$1.2M.** Everything that generates revenue or de-risks the build: land, 100% solar/water core, 28 capsule units, Pricewards sewing lab (revenue engine #1), lean clinic, village market, food production (goats + fish + farm), minimal AI room.

**Wave 2 (M30–M36) — revenue-funded, NOT in this ask:** fleet workshop (Sprynter servicing — see §7 critical dependency), music/film studios, conference rooms, 3D-print lab, capsule unit upgrades (build-up phase), housing expansion beyond 28.

**Why this split:** every Wave-1 item either (a) produces revenue within 12 months of completion, or (b) is a hard prerequisite (solar/water/title). Studios and heavy workshops cost more than they earn in their first year — defer them to occupancy revenue. This is the ship-and-iterate doctrine applied to capital.
